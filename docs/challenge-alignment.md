# Challenge alignment matrix

**Official source:** "Field Shift: Adapting Farms with NASA Data", 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge. https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/adapting-farms-with-nasa-data/ (verified 2026-10-30; see `docs/research/challenge-source.md`).

- The Resources tab says resources will be available on 13 Nov 2026, so there is no official resource list yet.
- The challenge page states no judging criteria.
- Rows C1–C8 are our faithful structuring of the challenge text, which is phrased as "may" and "may consider".

**Status values:**
- **Pending:** not started.
- **Foundation:** groundwork only; the requirement is not yet met.
- **Partial.**
- **Met.**
- **Not applicable.**

Last updated: Phase 1 (data research and live verification), 2026-10-30.
